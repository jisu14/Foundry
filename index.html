Simulation Assumptions & Model Boundaries

What this simulation models vs. what it cannot predict:

  • Price Sensitivity Curve: Conversions decrease non-linearly as unit price exceeds the target group's declared budget tolerance.
  • Channel Relevance: Ad impressions and click-through rates scale based on whether the chosen channel matches the audience's primary discovery habit.
  • What It Does NOT Prove: This model assumes immediate visibility without organic churn or viral decay. It does not validate real consumer willingness to buy, does not forecast actual commercial revenue, and cannot substitute for speaking directly with real human customers.
