Geographic Gaps

Country Coverage Disparities

While macroeconomic and telecom indicators cover 150+ economies, enterprise and fintech metrics have stark regional gaps:

  • Mobile Apps Data Pack (Eurostat): Official open statistics cover EU-27 and European partner states (e.g. DEU, FRA, NLD, SWE, ESP). Non-European economies (e.g. USA, IND, KEN, BRA) have preserved NULLs because global download and store spending metrics require commercial paid licenses (Sensor Tower / data.ai / Appfigures).
  • Enterprise ICT Adoption (UNCTAD): Primarily surveyed across European and high-income OECD economies. Developing and African nations lack harmonized enterprise website and e-order survey data.
  • Fixed Broadband >= 10 Mbps (ITU): Several developing island nations and lower-income economies report zero speed-tier breakdowns.
  • Zero Imputed Values: Foundry retains empty values as missing. No proxy numbers are hallucinated or copied between neighboring states.
Temporal Gaps

Reference Year Intervals

Different international agencies collect and publish data on non-annual cadences:

  • Global Findex Triennial Cycle: Surveyed only in 2011, 2014, 2017, 2021, and 2024. Intermediate years (e.g. 2018–2020, 2022–2023) have zero survey records.
  • UNCTAD E-Commerce Surveys: Enterprise survey frequencies vary between 1 and 3 years depending on national statistical offices.
  • Eurostat Digital Economy Cadence: Surveyed annually or biennially with harmonized questionnaires across European national statistical offices.
  • Lagged Reporting: Official vetted statistics have a 12-to-24 month publication lag. 2025/2026 data is currently uncollected by international bodies.

How Foundry Protects Your Field Discoveries

Customer interviews, pricing elasticity responses, and unreleased product feedback are confidential startup assets. Foundry treats your field research with institutional database isolation.

Storage Backend: PostgreSQL + RLS
Tenant Isolation: auth.uid() = user_id
Shared Model Opt-In: Explicit Affirmative
Raw Notes Exposure: 0% (Strictly Blocked)

1. Row Level Security (RLS) Isolation

Every record in public.founder_real_evidence is tagged with your user_id and workspace_id. PostgreSQL executes policies at the database engine level, guaranteeing that queries from any other user or anonymous caller will return zero rows.

2. De-Identification & Aggregation Firewall

If you explicitly check the opt-in box, Foundry's shared benchmark model accesses your record exclusively via public.shared_evidence_benchmarks. This SQL view strips:

  • Founder and Startup Identity (user_id, workspace_id)
  • Customer Group Names & Participant Demographics
  • Specific Research Questions & Test Inquiries
  • All Qualitative Quotes, Raw Interview Transcripts & Notes
  • Source Citations & Conductor Names

Only high-level numeric distributions (e.g., median conversion rate by industry vertical across ≥3 independent contributors) are retained to protect k-anonymity.

3. Export & Hard Deletion Sovereignty

You retain 100% intellectual property ownership. You can download your entire dataset at any time via JSON or CSV export, or permanently delete records via ON DELETE CASCADE with zero lingering retention or backup copies.

Foundry Public Beta Privacy & Data Ethics Policy

Version 2.0 (Public Beta)
Transparent disclosure on data storage, tenant isolation, AI provider usage, and open data reuse licenses.
Our Core Commitment to Founders:

Your venture hypotheses, pitch drafts, customer research, and simulation runs belong exclusively to you. Foundry never sells your data, never trains public models on private founder inputs, and isolates every workspace with PostgreSQL Row Level Security.

1. What Data We Collect & Store

  • Founder Account: Email address and authentication credentials managed securely via Supabase Auth.
  • Startup Hypotheses: Venture name, business model, stage, pricing, customer personas, competitor notes, and test budgets.
  • Deterministic Simulation Runs: Algorithmic conversions, sensitivity uncertainty intervals, and contextual macroeconomic snapshots.
  • Empirical Field Research: Founder customer interviews, survey sample sizes ($N$), observation rates, and methodological limitations. (Strictly private by default; shared benchmarks require affirmative opt-in with $N \ge 100$ de-identification gates).
  • Beta Feedback & Reports: Discrepancy reports and product suggestions submitted to improve the Foundry platform.

2. Workspace Isolation via PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS)

Every database table (founder_profiles, customer_groups, marketing_campaigns, simulation_runs, founder_real_evidence, ai_analyst_logs) enforces strict Row Level Security policies (auth.uid() = user_id). Database queries execute under authenticated tenant contexts, guaranteeing that no founder can view, modify, or delete another founder's workspace.

3. Proprietary Backend AI Architecture

⚠️ Google AI Studio Free Tier Notice:

Market research and customer persona simulations are processed through a secure server-side proxy. Founders' proprietary workspace hypotheses are never used for external model training.

Foundry's Strict Privacy Safeguards:
  • Minimal Data Shield: By default, only public macroeconomic indicators and your target country/vertical are sent. Your startup description and customer notes are withheld unless you check the affirmative consent box.
  • Zero Client Keys: All AI credentials and model secrets are stored strictly in server-side configuration and are never exposed to browser clients.
  • Zero AI Math: All simulation revenue, reach, and conversion calculations are performed in deterministic local code—never generated by AI.
  • No Synthetic Fact Invention: The AI intelligence engine is constrained to cite only verified indicators. If data is sparse, execution halts before calling the model.

4. Confirmed External Data Source Reuse Terms

All macroeconomic indicators in Foundry's Market Data Library originate from official statistical bodies and are published under confirmed open reuse licenses:

World Bank (WDI) & Global Findex:

Open Data license under CC-BY 4.0 International. Free for commercial and non-commercial redistribution with attribution.

International Telecommunication Union (ITU):

DataHub indicators republished under ITU non-commercial research terms with official source citations.

Eurostat (European Commission):

Commission Decision 2011/833/EU on the reuse of Commission documents. Open and free with mandatory attribution.

UN Trade and Development (UNCTADstat):

United Nations open statistical dissemination guidelines with explicit source citation.

Note on Commercial Intelligence (Sensor Tower, data.ai): Foundry does not redistribute or scrape proprietary commercial datasets. Any commercial metrics require authorized, user-provided license files.

5. Founder Rights: Data Portability & Right to Erasure

  • Data Portability: You can download a complete JSON archive of all your workspace data at any time by clicking Export Workspace (JSON).
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You can permanently delete your entire workspace at any time. When deleted, all profiles, simulations, and empirical research records are permanently removed from the database with zero retention.
  • Consent Revocation: You can revoke sharing consent for any empirical study at any time with immediate effect.
