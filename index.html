Geographic Gaps

Country Coverage Disparities

While macroeconomic and telecom indicators cover 150+ economies, enterprise and fintech metrics have stark regional gaps:

  • Mobile Apps Data Pack (Eurostat): Official open statistics cover EU-27 and European partner states (e.g. DEU, FRA, NLD, SWE, ESP). Non-European economies (e.g. USA, IND, KEN, BRA) have preserved NULLs because global download and store spending metrics require commercial paid licenses (Sensor Tower / data.ai / Appfigures).
  • Enterprise ICT Adoption (UNCTAD): Primarily surveyed across European and high-income OECD economies. Developing and African nations lack harmonized enterprise website and e-order survey data.
  • Fixed Broadband >= 10 Mbps (ITU): Several developing island nations and lower-income economies report zero speed-tier breakdowns.
  • Zero Imputed Values: Foundry retains empty values as missing. No proxy numbers are hallucinated or copied between neighboring states.
Temporal Gaps

Reference Year Intervals

Different international agencies collect and publish data on non-annual cadences:

  • Global Findex Triennial Cycle: Surveyed only in 2011, 2014, 2017, 2021, and 2024. Intermediate years (e.g. 2018–2020, 2022–2023) have zero survey records.
  • UNCTAD E-Commerce Surveys: Enterprise survey frequencies vary between 1 and 3 years depending on national statistical offices.
  • Eurostat Digital Economy Cadence: Surveyed annually or biennially with harmonized questionnaires across European national statistical offices.
  • Lagged Reporting: Official vetted statistics have a 12-to-24 month publication lag. 2025/2026 data is currently uncollected by international bodies.

Real Evidence Privacy & Data Security Architecture

How Foundry Protects Your Field Discoveries

Customer interviews, pricing elasticity responses, and unreleased product feedback are confidential startup assets. Foundry treats your field research with institutional database isolation.

Storage Backend: PostgreSQL + RLS
Tenant Isolation: auth.uid() = user_id
Shared Model Opt-In: Explicit Affirmative
Raw Notes Exposure: 0% (Strictly Blocked)

1. Row Level Security (RLS) Isolation

Every record in public.founder_real_evidence is tagged with your user_id and workspace_id. PostgreSQL executes policies at the database engine level, guaranteeing that queries from any other user or anonymous caller will return zero rows.

2. De-Identification & Aggregation Firewall

If you explicitly check the opt-in box, Foundry's shared benchmark model accesses your record exclusively via public.shared_evidence_benchmarks. This SQL view strips:

  • Founder and Startup Identity (user_id, workspace_id)
  • Customer Group Names & Participant Demographics
  • Specific Research Questions & Test Inquiries
  • All Qualitative Quotes, Raw Interview Transcripts & Notes
  • Source Citations & Conductor Names

Only high-level numeric distributions (e.g., median conversion rate by industry vertical across ≥3 independent contributors) are retained to protect k-anonymity.

3. Export & Hard Deletion Sovereignty

You retain 100% intellectual property ownership. You can download your entire dataset at any time via JSON or CSV export, or permanently delete records via ON DELETE CASCADE with zero lingering retention or backup copies.
